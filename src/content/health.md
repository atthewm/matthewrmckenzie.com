# Health and Fitness

I train for strength and endurance, and I treat recovery as part of the training, not an afterthought.

## Training

Most weeks are built around a strength split: back, chest, legs, shoulders, and arms and abs. The splits I use are in the [Health and Fitness folder](/?open=health) on the desktop.

## Recovery and Sleep

I track sleep, strain, and recovery with WHOOP and Eight Sleep. The data helps me decide when to push and when to back off, which matters more than any single workout.

## Nutrition

I log what I eat and track macros against daily targets. The foods I log most, with average macros per serving, are in [Top Foods](/?open=top-foods). A lot of what I cook is built around protein, which is where the [Cooking and Baking](/kitchen) section comes in.

## Tennis

I play social tennis. My racket and string setup is in [Hobbies](/?open=hobbies), down to the tension.
