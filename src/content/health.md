# Health and Fitness

I train for strength and endurance, and I treat recovery as part of the training.

## Training

Most weeks I run a strength split: back, chest, legs, shoulders, and arms and abs. The splits I use are in the [Health and Fitness folder](/desktop?open=health) on the desktop.

## Recovery and Sleep

I track sleep, strain, and recovery with WHOOP and Eight Sleep. The numbers tell me when to push and when to back off. Getting that call right matters more to me than any single workout.

## Nutrition

I log what I eat and track macros against daily targets. The foods I log most, with average macros per serving, are in [Top Foods](/desktop?open=top-foods). You can also play with them in the [Food Room](/food-room). A lot of what I cook is built around protein, and that is where [Cooking and Baking](/kitchen) comes in.

## Tennis

I play social tennis. My racket and string setup is in [Hobbies](/hobbies).
