Transcribed from the handwritten family recipe card. Flour is amended per the card's annotation to 1⅓ cups for a slightly thicker cookie.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup butter (1 stick), softened
- 6 tbsp brown sugar (⅜ cup, packed)
- 6 tbsp white sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp vanilla
- 1⅓ cups flour (~167 g)
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ½ cup English walnuts (optional)
- ½ cup chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Cream butter with brown and white sugar until light.
3. Beat in egg and vanilla.
4. Whisk flour, salt, and soda together; add to the wet mixture and mix just until combined.
5. Fold in chocolate chips and walnuts if using.
6. Drop by rounded tablespoon onto ungreased sheets, about 2 inches apart.
7. Bake at 375°F for about 8 minutes. Cool on the sheet 2 minutes before transferring.

## Notes
- The original card notes doubling conversions in parentheses: ⅜ cup brown sugar, ¾ tsp vanilla, 2 cups + 2 tbsp flour, ¾ tsp salt for a double batch.
- Flour here is 1⅓ cups, up from the card's original 1 cup + 2 tbsp.
- 8 minutes at 375°F produces a soft center; pull at 7 for chewier, 9 to 10 for crisp edges.
