Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card, the original Crisco-crust version. A whipped, French silk-style chocolate filling set in a blind-baked crust and topped with whipped cream.

## Ingredients

### Crust
- 1½ cups flour
- ½ cup Crisco (regular)
- Dash salt
- ¼ cup milk

### Filling
- ½ cup butter
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 squares (2 oz) unsweetened chocolate, melted (or cocoa + oil)
- 2 eggs
- ½ pint whipping cream
- Chocolate shavings, for topping

## Instructions

### Crust
1. Mix flour, Crisco, and salt, then add milk.
2. Bake at 425°F for 6 to 8 minutes, or until light brown (9 minutes total).
3. Cool completely before filling.

### Filling
1. Cream butter, sugar, and melted chocolate.
2. Beat until no sugar grains remain (5 to 10 minutes).
3. Add one egg and beat 5 minutes more.
4. Add the other egg.
5. Add vanilla.
6. Pour into the cooled crust and refrigerate until hard.
7. Add whipped cream to the top and finish with chocolate shavings.

## Notes
- The filling uses raw eggs and is never cooked. Use pasteurized eggs if that matters.

### Fat swap: ghee instead of Crisco
The card calls for ½ cup Crisco in the crust. To swap to ghee:
- Substitute by weight, not volume: ½ cup shortening is about 103 g, so use 103 g ghee (~7½ tbsp).
- Chill the ghee solid before cutting it in. Ghee has no water, so the crust stays tender but browns faster; check at 5 minutes.
- Trade-off: less flaky than shortening, more flavor.
- Macro impact is negligible.
