From Liz N., transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. A moist, oil-based carrot cake baked in a 9x13 and finished with cream cheese frosting.

## Ingredients

### Cake
- 1½ cups cooking oil
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1½ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups grated carrots

### Frosting
- 1 8-oz package cream cheese
- 1 stick butter
- 1 box powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions
1. Combine oil, sugar, and eggs. Beat together.
2. Sift all dry ingredients and mix in.
3. Stir in carrots.
4. Grease and flour a 9x13 pan. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes. For round pans, bake 35 to 37 minutes.
5. Combine frosting ingredients until smooth and frost the cooled cake.

## Notes
- Card annotation: for a round cake, make 1½ times the frosting.

### Fat swap: avocado oil
The card calls for 1½ cups "cooking oil" (canola or vegetable). Avocado oil is a direct 1:1 volume replacement with neutral flavor and no texture change in an oil-based cake. Macro impact is none; both are about 120 kcal and 14 g fat per tbsp. Ghee is not a good swap here, since this cake depends on liquid fat for its crumb.
