A cottage cheese and egg white base that delivers about 52 g of protein in the crust alone. Low fat, mostly whole food, and built to pair with a lean protein topping.

## Ingredients
- 226 g Daisy 2% cottage cheese
- 6 egg whites
- 60 g oat flour
- 1 scoop whey isolate (unflavored or neutral flavor; ProMix)
- 4 g baking powder
- 2 g garlic powder
- 2 g Italian seasoning
- 2 g salt

## Instructions
1. **Preheat oven to 425°F.** Line a baking sheet or pizza stone with parchment paper.
2. **Blend the cottage cheese smooth.** Add cottage cheese to a blender or food processor and blend until completely smooth, with no curds. Don't skip this or the texture will be grainy.
3. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk together oat flour, whey isolate, baking powder, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, and salt in a large bowl.
4. **Combine wet into dry.** Add the blended cottage cheese and egg whites to the dry mix. Stir until a thick, slightly sticky dough forms. If too wet to handle, add 1 tbsp oat flour at a time.
5. **Press the dough.** Transfer to parchment, wet your hands lightly, and press into a ~12-inch round about ¼ inch thick. Edges can be slightly thicker.
6. **Par-bake the crust** for 12 to 14 minutes until the top looks dry and the edges just start to brown.
7. **Top and finish.** Add toppings (sauce, cheese, protein) and return to the oven for 8 to 10 more minutes until the cheese is bubbly and the crust is golden.

## Notes
- **Topping strategy:** The crust is lean at 6 g fat. Part-skim mozzarella, a light sauce, and a lean meat (chicken or ground turkey) keep the full pizza in a sensible macro range. Roasted vegetable toppings add fiber cheaply.
- **Texture:** Expect a flatbread or naan texture, not crispy New York style. For more crispiness, par-bake longer (15 minutes) or use a pizza stone. Adding 1 tsp psyllium husk to the dry mix improves structure and adds ~3 g fiber.
- **Meal prep:** Par-baked crusts keep refrigerated for 3 days. Make 2 at once and wrap the second.
- **Whey optional:** Swap the whey scoop for 2 extra tbsp oat flour to keep it purely whole food. The cottage cheese and egg whites carry the protein load either way.
