A lighter, high-protein brownie built on Dutch cocoa, micellar casein, skyr, and whipped egg whites folded in for lift. About 46 calories and 4 g protein per piece. Grams are primary; weigh everything.

**Pan:** 9x13, parchment with overhang. **Oven:** 300°F. **Bake:** 18 to 21 minutes convection; 28 to 34 minutes conventional. **Equipment:** two bowls, hand mixer.

## Ingredients

### Dry
- 70 g Dutch-process cocoa (processed with alkali)
- 65 g micellar casein, unflavored (ProMix)
- 120 g Besti Powdered
- 50 g rye flour
- 10 g psyllium (ground fine if whole husks)
- 4 g fine salt
- 2 to 3 g stevia

### Wet
- 30 g unsalted butter, browned (melted also works)
- 170 g Siggi's nonfat plain
- 60 g honey
- 100 g whole eggs (2 large)
- 8 g vanilla

### Whites
- 250 g egg whites, room temperature
- 1.5 g (½ tsp) cream of tartar

## Instructions
1. Oven to 300°F. Line the 9x13.
2. Brown the butter over medium until the solids turn amber and smell nutty (or just melt it).
3. Bowl 1: whisk all the dry ingredients together until one even color, with no streaks or clumps.
4. Add all the wet ingredients to Bowl 1 and stir until smooth. It will be thick like frosting.
5. Bowl 2: whip the whites and cream of tartar to soft-firm peaks (tips curl over, not dry).
6. Scoop about a third of the whites into Bowl 1 and stir firmly to loosen.
7. Add the rest of the whites and fold gently until no white streaks remain.
8. Pour into the pan, spread level, and bake: 18 to 21 minutes convection (check at 16), or 28 to 34 minutes conventional. Done when the center springs back and a toothpick shows moist crumbs. Tent with foil at ~20 minutes if the top is darkening.
9. Cool 60 minutes, chill 30 minutes, then cut 6 x 5 for 30 pieces.
10. Storage: fridge, airtight, 4 to 5 days. Freeze the rest by day 2: wrap each piece, then a freezer bag, up to 3 months. Thaw wrapped (fridge overnight or 30 to 60 minutes on the counter). Do not refreeze.

## Notes
- **Changes from v1:** no topping, no espresso, Dutch cocoa instead of natural cocoa plus soda, all dry ingredients pre-whisked in one bowl, cream of tartar in the whites, 65 g casein, and 250 g whites. Full casein; clear whey was ruled out.
- Dutch cocoa with no espresso took care of the bitterness from v1.
- In a convection oven at 300°F, the first batch was done at about 21 minutes. Check at 16 and pull at moist crumbs. 275°F convection is worth trying.
- Stevia is zero calories and does not change the macros.
