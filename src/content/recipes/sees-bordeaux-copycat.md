A copycat of the See's Bordeaux: a soft brown sugar butter cream center dipped in milk chocolate and topped with chocolate sprinkles. Adapted from the [Self Proclaimed Foodie copycat](https://selfproclaimedfoodie.com/copycat-sees-bordeaux/), with the coffee removed (See's Bordeaux does not use it) and milk chocolate for the closest match. Grams are primary; weigh everything.

## Ingredients

### Center
- 113 g unsalted butter (8 tbsp)
- 213 g golden brown sugar, packed (1 cup). C&H preferred; other brands can turn grainy
- 60 g heavy cream (¼ cup)
- 0.75 g fine salt (⅛ tsp)
- 240 g powdered sugar, sifted (2 cups)

### Coating
- 340 g milk chocolate, chopped (12 oz). Dark works if preferred
- 14 g unsalted butter (1 tbsp)
- ~30 g chocolate sprinkles or jimmies (2 tbsp)

## Instructions
1. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium-high heat, melt the 113 g butter with the 213 g brown sugar. Once boiling, boil 2 minutes.
2. Stir in the 60 g cream and the salt. Return to a boil and cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly. Remove from heat.
3. Cool in the pan 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the 240 g powdered sugar until a smooth, uniform dough forms.
5. Cover and refrigerate until it holds shape like play dough, about 2 hours.
6. Portion into ~15 g pieces. Roll into balls, then press the base flat for the See's mound shape. Set on a parchment-lined sheet.
7. Freeze 10 minutes.
8. Melt the 340 g chocolate with the 14 g butter at 50% power in 1-minute bursts, stirring after each.
9. Dip each center with a fork, tap off the excess, and set on parchment. Add sprinkles on top before the chocolate sets.
10. Let set fully at room temperature or in the fridge.

## Notes
- Storage: airtight at room temperature up to 1 week, fridge up to 2 weeks, freezer up to 2 months. Bring frozen pieces to room temperature before opening the container so condensation does not dull the chocolate.
- Optional: 1 tsp instant coffee with the cream for depth. Not in the See's original.
