Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. One 9x5x3 loaf of walnut banana bread, finished with melted butter and cinnamon-sugar while still warm.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup butter (or margarine)
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 3 large bananas, mashed
- 1 cup chopped nuts (English walnuts)
- 1 tbsp melted butter (for the top)
- Cinnamon-sugar mixture (for the top)

## Instructions
1. Cream butter and sugar. Beat in eggs, one at a time.
2. Mix in the sifted dry ingredients.
3. Beat in mashed banana. Add nuts.
4. Pour into a greased and floured 9x5x3-inch pan.
5. Bake at 350°F for 50 to 60 minutes. For 3 mini loaves, bake 37 minutes.
6. While the bread is still warm, pour melted butter over the top and sprinkle with cinnamon-sugar.
