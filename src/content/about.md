# About

I raise capital for real estate. I am Senior Vice President, Capital Formation at Menlo Equities, where I lead capital formation across the firm's private wealth channel. Away from work I train, pay attention to recovery, and cook.

## Work

At Menlo Equities I build and grow relationships with high net worth and ultra high net worth investors, family offices, and registered investment advisors across the firm's investment platforms.

Before joining Menlo in 2026, I spent nearly a decade at Civitas Capital Group, most recently as Vice President, Investor Relations. There I raised capital from private wealth investors across closed end funds, open end perpetual vehicles, and co-investments. I managed relationships across North America, the Middle East, and Asia.

How I work is easy to describe. I underwrite thoroughly, I communicate clearly, and I build partnerships meant to last longer than any single deal. There is more on the [Work](/work) page.

## Health and Fitness

I train for strength and endurance, and I take recovery seriously. I track sleep, training load, and nutrition, and I use the numbers to decide how hard to push on a given day. I also play social tennis. There is more on the [Health and Fitness](/health) page.

## Cooking and Baking

I bake sourdough from my own rye starter. I make jam. I work out high protein versions of everyday meals. I write every recipe down properly so it comes out the same the next time. See [Cooking and Baking](/kitchen).

## Elsewhere

I studied economics, public policy, political science, and photography at Southern Methodist University. I live in Austin, Texas.
