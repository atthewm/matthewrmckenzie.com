# About

I raise capital for real estate. I am Senior Vice President, Capital Formation at Menlo Equities, where I lead capital formation across the firm's private wealth channel. Outside of work, most of my time goes to training, recovery, and the kitchen.

## Work

At Menlo Equities I develop and expand relationships with high net worth and ultra high net worth investors, family offices, and registered investment advisors across the firm's investment platforms.

Before joining Menlo in 2026, I spent nearly a decade at Civitas Capital Group, most recently as Vice President, Investor Relations. I raised capital from private wealth investors across closed end funds, open end perpetual vehicles, and co-investments, and managed relationships across North America, the Middle East, and Asia.

The short version of how I work: thorough underwriting, clear communication, and partnerships built to last longer than any single deal. More on the [Work](/work) page.

## Health and Fitness

I train for strength and endurance and pay close attention to recovery. I track sleep, training load, and nutrition, and I use that data to decide how hard to push on a given day. I also play social tennis. More on the [Health and Fitness](/health) page.

## Cooking and Baking

I bake sourdough from my own rye starter, make jam, and develop high protein versions of everyday meals. Every recipe is written down properly so the results repeat. See [Cooking and Baking](/kitchen).

## Elsewhere

I studied economics, public policy, political science, and photography at Southern Methodist University. I live in Austin, Texas.
