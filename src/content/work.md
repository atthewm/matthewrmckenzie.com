# Work

**$800M+** in equity capital raised across PE real estate strategies. **200+** active investor relationships: family offices, UHNW individuals, RIAs, and institutional allocators. **10+ years** in capital formation, from analyst to VP. ## Principles

A few things I will not compromise on:

1. Thoughtful underwriting over speed. The best capital relationships are built on rigor.
2. Alignment between capital partners and operators. Structures should reward patience and shared outcomes.
3. Clear communication. Investors deserve direct answers, especially when the news is mixed.
4. Durable business models. I focus on companies built for long term ownership.
