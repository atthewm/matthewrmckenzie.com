# Work

I lead private wealth capital formation at Menlo Equities. My work is building durable partnerships between investors and real estate operators, with disciplined underwriting and long-term alignment at the center.

## By the Numbers

**$800M+** in equity capital raised across PE real estate strategies. **200+** active investor relationships spanning family offices, UHNW individuals, RIAs, and institutional allocators. **10+ years** of progressive capital formation experience, from analyst to SVP. Raised capital across closed end funds, open end vehicles, institutional separate accounts, and single asset syndications in major U.S. markets, Asia, and the Middle East.

## Principles

I hold a few things as non negotiable in how I work:

1. Thoughtful underwriting over speed. The best capital relationships are built on rigor, not urgency.
2. Alignment between capital partners and operators. Structures should reward patience and shared outcomes.
3. Clarity in communication. Investors deserve direct answers, especially when the news is mixed.
4. Durable business models. I focus on companies built for long term ownership, not quick exits.
