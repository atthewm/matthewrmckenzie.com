Adapted from the classic Cuisinart booklet strawberry ice cream, upgraded with a light 2-yolk custard base for a silkier, firmer texture: richer than the eggless original, lighter than a full French-style frozen custard. The vanilla is split, half infused pod and half extract.

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups fresh strawberries, hulled (~250 g)
- ¾ cup whole milk
- ⅔ cup granulated sugar
- 2 large egg yolks
- ½ vanilla bean pod, split lengthwise and seeds scraped
- Pinch of salt
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- ¾ tsp pure vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Mash the strawberries to your preferred texture. Cover and refrigerate. These go in raw at the end, never into the custard.
2. Split the vanilla pod and scrape the seeds. Add seeds and pod to a saucepan with the milk and half the sugar. Warm over medium until steaming, just below a simmer (~150°F). Off heat, steep 10 to 15 minutes.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the remaining sugar until pale and slightly thickened (~30 seconds).
4. Remove the pod. Rewarm the milk if it dropped below ~150°F. Slowly pour half into the yolks, whisking constantly. Return everything to the saucepan.
5. Cook over medium-low, stirring constantly and scraping the bottom, until it coats the back of a spoon and reaches 170 to 175°F. Do not boil.
6. Immediately strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean bowl. Off heat, stir in the salt, heavy cream, and vanilla extract. Stir in the reserved strawberries with all their juices.
7. Cool over an ice bath, then refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight until thoroughly cold.
8. Churn in the ice cream maker 15 to 20 minutes to soft-serve texture.
9. For firm scoops, transfer to an airtight container and freeze ~2 hours. Rest 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes
- **Temperature is the whole game.** Past ~180°F the yolks curdle. A thermometer is strongly recommended; otherwise pull the moment a finger swipe on the spoon leaves a clean line.
- **Never skip the strain.** It catches any stray cooked egg.
- **Why split the vanilla:** pod oils infuse into the fat during heating (deep, floral notes); extract goes in off heat because its volatile aromatics cook off.
- **Keep strawberries out of the pot** so their fresh flavor and color survive.
- For a firmer freezer texture without more yolks, swap 1 to 2 tbsp of the sugar for light corn syrup. It suppresses ice crystals.
- The freezer bowl must be frozen solid 16 to 24 hours before churning.
- Save the spent pod: rinse, dry, and bury it in a jar of sugar.
