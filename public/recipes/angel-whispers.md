Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. Tiny cornstarch cookies sandwiched with lemon butter and rolled in powdered sugar.

## Ingredients

### Lemon butter
- 1 egg, beaten lightly
- 1 lemon rind, grated
- ⅔ cup sugar
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1½ tbsp softened butter

### Cookie mixture
- ¾ cup cake flour, plus 2 tbsp
- ¾ cup cornstarch
- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp baking powder
- Lemon or almond extract to taste (about ⅛ tsp)
- Powdered sugar, for rolling

## Instructions
1. Make the lemon butter: blend the egg, lemon rind, sugar, lemon juice, and butter and mix well. Cook over low heat until thick.
2. Beat eggs well.
3. Add sugar, butter, and the flour/cornstarch mixture that has been sifted with the baking powder. Add flavoring.
4. Drop on a greased cookie sheet in tiny drops, about the size of a brazil nut.
5. Bake in a hot oven (375°F) about 4 minutes. The card notes 8 minutes in the Miele.
6. Fill cookies with lemon butter and roll in powdered sugar.

## Notes
- The card gives no yield. Estimates assume ~48 tiny cookies making 24 sandwiches and ~⅓ cup powdered sugar picked up in rolling. Count and adjust.
