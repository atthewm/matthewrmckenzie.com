From Mom, transcribed from the recipe binder. A classic double-crust apple pie with a simple butter-shortening pastry.

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 5 large Granny Smith apples, quartered and sliced from the short end
- ½ lemon, squeezed over the apples
- 1 full cup sugar
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp flour
- Pats of butter (for dotting the filling)
- Sugar, for sprinkling the top crust

### 2-crust pastry
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- ⅔ cup butter shortening
- 2 tbsp chilled butter
- 4 tbsp water (ok to add a 5th)

## Instructions
1. Cut up the apples. Squeeze lemon juice over them.
2. Mix together sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon, and flour.
3. Mix the crust and fit the bottom crust into the pie plate. Sprinkle flour on it.
4. Add the apple mixture and dot with pats of butter.
5. Add the top crust. Sprinkle sugar on top and cut slits in the crust.
6. Bake at 400°F for 10 minutes, then 350°F for about 1 hour.

## Notes

### Fat swap: ghee instead of shortening
The card calls for ⅔ cup butter shortening in the pastry. To swap to ghee:
- Substitute by weight: ⅔ cup shortening is about 137 g, so use 137 g ghee.
- Chill ghee solid, cube it, and work fast. A double crust is more forgiving than a single since the top crust hides any unevenness.
- Keep the 2 tbsp chilled butter as written. It is already doing the flavor work.
- Expect slightly shorter, less layered pastry. Add the 5th tbsp of water if the dough reads dry.
- Macro impact is negligible.
