Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. A classic loaf seasoned with onion, green pepper, bay, and thyme, baked at 350°F for about 45 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lbs ground beef
- 1½ cups coarse dry bread crumbs
- ½ cup finely chopped onion
- 2 tbsp chopped green pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 medium bay leaf, crushed
- Dash thyme
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 8-oz can seasoned tomato sauce

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine the first 7 ingredients (beef through thyme).
3. Add eggs and tomato sauce; mix well.
4. Shape into a loaf and bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes.

## Notes
- **Sodium is the constraint here.** 2 tsp salt plus seasoned tomato sauce puts one serving at ~57% of a 2,300 mg daily target. Cutting salt to ¾ tsp and using no-salt-added tomato sauce drops it to ~450 mg per serving.
- Swapping to 93/7 beef takes a serving to ~340 kcal / 14 g fat with no protein loss.
