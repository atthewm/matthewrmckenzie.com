Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. A cooked-down cocoa sauce that boils for half an hour once it comes up, so it reduces considerably.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- ⅓ cup cocoa
- Pinch salt
- ⅓ cup cream
- ⅓ cup milk
- 1 tbsp Karo light syrup

Added after cooking:
- 1 tbsp butter
- ⅛ tsp vanilla

## Instructions
1. Combine sugar, cocoa, salt, cream, milk, and Karo syrup in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil, then cook ½ hour from the point it starts to boil.
3. Off heat, stir in the butter and vanilla.

## Notes
- Yield is an estimate. A half-hour boil reduces this to roughly 1½ cups, but the card does not say. Measure the finished sauce; per-tablespoon macros shift accordingly while the batch total stays fixed.
