Transcribed from Anne McKenzie's handwritten recipe card. A pressed butter crust topped with cherry pie filling and a pecan crumble, finished with an almond glaze. Makes a 13x9 pan of 24 bars.

## Ingredients

### Crust and crumble
- 3 cups all-purpose flour (reserve 1 cup of the finished mixture)
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- ½ tsp table salt
- 1½ cups (12 oz) cold butter, cubed
- ¾ cup chopped pecans

### Filling
- 3 cups canned cherry pie filling (21 oz)

### Glaze
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 4 to 5 tsp whole milk
- ¼ tsp almond extract

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line bottom and sides of a 13x9 pan with heavy-duty foil, allowing 2 to 3 inches to extend over the sides. Lightly grease the foil with cooking spray.
2. Pulse flour, sugar, and salt in a food processor until combined. Add butter cubes and pulse until crumbly.
3. Reserve 1 cup of the flour mixture. Press the remaining mixture onto the bottom of the prepared pan.
4. Bake 25 to 30 minutes until lightly browned.
5. Toss the reserved flour mixture together with the pecans.
6. Spread cherry pie filling over the crust and sprinkle the pecan mixture over the filling.
7. Bake 40 to 45 minutes until golden brown.
8. Cool in pan 1 hour. Lift foil out of the pan.
9. Stir together powdered sugar, milk, and almond extract; drizzle over the pecan mixture.
