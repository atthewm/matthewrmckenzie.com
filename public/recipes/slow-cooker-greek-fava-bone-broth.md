Creamy Greek fava made from split vetches (Arosis Greek Organic Fava of Vetches), slow cooked in chicken bone broth into a high-protein carb base. It cooks like split peas: no soak, and it breaks down into a purée.

## Ingredients
- 340 g Arosis fava of vetches (full bag), rinsed
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced (optional)
- 6 cups chicken bone broth
- 1 bay leaf
- Juice of ½ lemon (at finish)
- Salt to taste at the end (the broth carries sodium)
- Avocado oil spray, for the sauté

## Instructions
1. **Sauté the onion** until soft and lightly golden, about 8 minutes, adding the garlic in the last minute. A light mist of avocado oil is all it needs.
2. **Load the slow cooker** with the sautéed onion, vetches, broth, and bay leaf. Stir once.
3. **Slow cook** on LOW 6 to 8 hours (or HIGH 3 to 4) until fully broken down and creamy. Stir once or twice if you are around; add water if too thick.
4. **Finish.** Remove the bay leaf, stir in the lemon juice, salt to taste, and whisk vigorously until silky. It thickens as it cools.

## Notes
- Classic toppings: lemon, capers, raw red onion.
- Reheats well; thin with water or broth.
- Portion by cooked weight.
