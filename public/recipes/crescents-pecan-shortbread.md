Transcribed from the handwritten recipe card. Buttery pecan shortbread shaped into crescents and rolled in powdered sugar straight from the oven.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 5 tbsp sugar
- 1 cup pecans, finely chopped
- 1 tsp water
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- Powdered sugar, for rolling

## Instructions
1. Cream butter thoroughly.
2. Gradually work in flour, sugar, and nuts.
3. Add vanilla and water.
4. Cool the dough, then shape into crescents.
5. Bake at 350°F for about 20 minutes, or until edges turn slightly brown.
6. Roll in powdered sugar immediately.

## Notes
- The card gives no yield. 36 is an estimate; adjust if you count them.
